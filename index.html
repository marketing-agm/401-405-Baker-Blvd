Dear 401 Baker LLC and 405 Baker LLC,

Thank you for the opportunity to submit a proposal for Management Services for 401 and 405 Baker Blvd in Tukwila.

Changing managers is a significant decision, and AGM’s aim is to make the transition simple for Ownership. We coordinate the handover with the current manager directly, notify the tenants and take custody of the leases, deposits, keys and records, so Ownership does not have to step into day-to-day operations to make the change.

From the first month, Ownership will see more of its buildings than it has before. Each property receives its own financial package in the Owner Portal by the 15th, with every paid invoice attached, and Ownership can view rent collections, tenant balances, maintenance records and lease documents at any time.

In the first thirty days AGM reviews every lease and the NNN charges billed under it, to confirm that every expense the leases allow is being recovered. AGM’s building engineer then puts both buildings on a preventative maintenance schedule, so roofs, heating, fire sprinklers and doors are serviced on a calendar rather than when they fail.

AGM’s management approach is grounded in the protection and growth of invested capital. We operate each asset with fiduciary discipline by controlling operating expenses, providing transparent monthly financial reporting through our Owner Portal, and preserving physical condition through proactive maintenance and capital planning. The result for Ownership is two well-maintained assets, predictable costs, and a single accountable point of contact.

We appreciate the opportunity to be considered and look forward to working with you.

Sincerely,

Troy Gessel

Principal

  • Tenant insurance certificates collected and checked against the lease limits

Day 31–60

  • Vendor contracts and fire, backflow and HVAC service confirmed current, and rebid where pricing is off market
  • Assessed values reviewed against the King County appeal deadline, with a recommendation
Day 61–90

  • Reserve levels and spending approval thresholds agreed
